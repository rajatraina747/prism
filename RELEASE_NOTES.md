## What's New

Prism has a new interface: fewer pages, easier to read, and a player that does more.

- **One Downloads page.** The Dashboard and Transfers are now a single home
  page: add a link, see your totals, and follow what's downloading. Your most
  recent downloads appear there while the list is short.
- **The Library is now a media library.** Posters are the default view, and a
  torrent's files open straight from its card. Statistics moved into the
  Library as an Activity strip, where you can pick the period.
- **Needs attention.** Failed and cancelled downloads now live under this
  filter on Downloads instead of in the Library.
- **Settings has five tabs and a search box,** and About and the legal pages
  are now inside it.
- **Player:** Previous and Next through a folder or playlist, recent files,
  audio delay, frame step, screenshots, A-B loop, picture shape, and a shortcut
  sheet.
- **Open With Prism (macOS):** open video and audio files from Finder. Files
  opened together become a playlist. Prism doesn't make itself the default app.
- **Torrents:** the file picker shows what you've selected against the free
  space, and Prism checks there's room before it starts.
- **Forget playback history** clears what the player remembers.
- **Smaller windows:** below 1000px wide, the sidebar shrinks to icons.

### Fixes

- **The subtitle and Play a File pickers now open attached to the player.** The
  picker could appear on the first click but not respond, with clicks going
  through to the video.
- **Text is easier to read:** one set of type sizes, nothing below 12px, and
  stronger contrast for secondary text.
