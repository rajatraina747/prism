## What's New

### Audio

- **Choose the audio quality.** Audio-only downloads now offer Original (the
  site's own audio, untouched: the best quality there is, and the fastest),
  MP3 at best/320/256/192/128 kbps, M4A or Opus. Pick it per download, or for
  a whole batch or playlist; Settings → Downloads sets the default, and your
  current format carries over.
- **See what the site actually serves.** The details show the source, such as
  "Source: Opus 106 kbps", and warn when a choice only makes a bigger file:
  a 320 kbps MP3 of a 106 kbps source sounds no better.
